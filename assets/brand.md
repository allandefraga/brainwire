# Brainwire identity

Brainwire uses a connected five-node mark with a restrained cobalt accent and
an outlined wordmark. The icon remains readable without depending on a font or
a network request. Use the light or dark wordmark to match the background.

- `brainwire-logo.svg`: light wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `brainwire-logo-light.svg`: dark wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `brainwire-icon.svg`: compact mark and favicon.

Keep sufficient contrast and space around the mark. The wordmark artwork was
set from Geist 1.800 at weight 650 and converted to paths. Font copyright:
2024 The Geist Project Authors; SIL Open Font License 1.1.
[Upstream font and license](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font).

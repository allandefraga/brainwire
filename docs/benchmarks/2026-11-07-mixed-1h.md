# One-hour mixed load and restart

A local beta.3 candidate ran a synthetic standalone graph with 100,000 nodes
and 100,000 relationships, a 2 GiB container limit and two CPUs. One writer and
one reader ran for 3,600 seconds on a shared host. The candidate image predates
the later Cypher alias and Raft advisory; it is not the final release image.

The run completed 87,021 committed batches, 21,755,250 node/relationship
update rows and 70,030 successful reader iterations. Each batch updates both
the node and its edge. The harness verified each of the 100,000 pairs before
and after SIGKILL and restart; the offline graph audit passed. It sampled RSS
357 times: the highest sample was 435 MiB; the cgroup peak was 589 MiB. No
cgroup OOM kill was reported. These are measurements of one local run, not a
process-wide RSS ceiling, kernel-log verification or a capacity guarantee.

The runner revision used here recorded aggregate totals, not progress in each
time window. It cannot prove uninterrupted availability. SIGKILL happened after
acknowledged writes and quiescent verification; lost acknowledgements,
power/disk loss, quorum and multi-machine failures were not tested. A later
runner revision adds progress windows; this receipt does not establish them.

The executed candidate command used `--rows 100000 --batch 250 --seconds 3600
--deadline 4200 --memory 2 --sample-seconds 10 --pause 0.02`. Use the current
runner instructions for a new test; its acceptance criteria now differ.

After the Cypher and Raft changes, a separate two-minute run of the final
candidate with 10,000 nodes verified progress in each 60-second window, then
checked every pair after SIGKILL and restart. It recorded 3,376 committed
batches and 3,203 successful reader iterations. This shorter check does not
extend the one-hour evidence to the final binary. [Final candidate receipt](2026-11-07-mixed-final-2m.json).
